HOW TO APPLY FOR OUTGOING EXCHANGE:
1. Contact the Auslandsbeauftragter, Internationalisierungs-Coach, or faculty patron
2. Submit application via ISO (International Student Office)
3. Apply for funding: Erasmus+ grant or DAAD PROMOS scholarship
4. Select courses abroad
5. Talk to PAV for credit recognition
6. Complete Learning Agreement
7. Consider a language course at Sprachenzentrum first
