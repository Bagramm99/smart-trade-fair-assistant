LANGUAGE COURSE OFFER — OSTFALIA SPRACHENZENTRUM

The Language Centre offers courses in many languages during the semester.

AVAILABLE LANGUAGES (vary by semester):
- German as a Foreign Language (DaF) — multiple levels A1–C1
- English — Business English, Academic English, general English
- French — various levels
- Spanish — various levels
- Other languages depending on demand

HOW TO REGISTER:
Registration opens at the start of each semester. Places are limited — register early.
Registration via the Ostfalia student portal or directly at the Language Centre.

COURSE TIMES:
Courses run alongside regular study timetables. Check the current timetable at the Language Centre website.

INTENSIVE COURSES (SEMESTER BREAKS):
Additional intensive courses are offered during the lecture-free period.
Particularly useful for international students wanting to improve German before or during their stay.

FEES:
Most courses are free for enrolled Ostfalia students. Some specialist courses may have a fee.
Guest students (Gasthörer) pay a guest auditor fee.
