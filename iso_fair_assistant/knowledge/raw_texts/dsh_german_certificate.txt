DSH EXAM AND PREPARATION COURSE

The DSH (Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang) is the German language test required for university admission.

WHAT IS THE DSH?
The DSH tests your German language skills at university level. Successfully passing DSH-2 or DSH-3 fulfils the language requirement for degree programmes at Ostfalia.

DSH PREPARATION COURSE:
Ostfalia offers a DSH preparation course to help you reach the required level.
The course prepares you for all parts of the DSH exam: listening, reading, writing, and oral exam.

EXAM DATES:
DSH exams are held at specific dates each semester. Check the Language Centre for current exam schedule.

WHO SHOULD TAKE THE DSH?
- International applicants who do not yet have a recognised German language certificate
- Students who want to use the DSH to fulfil the language requirement for their degree programme
