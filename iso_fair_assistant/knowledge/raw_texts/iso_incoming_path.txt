TOPIC: Step-by-step path for incoming international students at Ostfalia
============================================================

YOUR WAY TO OSTFALIA — STEP BY STEP

Step 1 — Nomination
Contact your home university's international office. They nominate you via email to: incoming-iso@ostfalia.de

Step 2 — Application and acceptance
ISO sends you an acceptance letter and enrolment instructions.
Enrolment is handled by the Studierenden-Service / Immatrikulationsbüro.

Step 3 — Before arrival
- Arrange health insurance (mandatory for all students in Germany)
- Apply for student housing via Studierendenwerk: stw-on.de
- Apply for a visa if required (non-EU students)
- Complete your Learning Agreement with your home university
- Consider registering for a German language course at the Sprachenzentrum

Step 4 — On arrival
- Register your address (Anmeldung) within 2 weeks: Stadthaus, Stadtmarkt 4, Wolfenbüttel
- Validate your student card at the Ostfalia Rechenzentrum
- Attend the international orientation event organised by ISO

Step 5 — During your stay
- Keep your Learning Agreement updated if course selection changes
- Contact ISO for any questions: incoming-iso@ostfalia.de
- Participate in the FYI integration programme for support and social activities

Step 6 — End of stay
- Request your Transcript of Records from the Prüfungsamt
- Return library materials and clear your account
- Deregister your address (Abmeldung) at the Einwohnermeldeamt
