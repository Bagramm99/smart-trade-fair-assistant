PATHWAYS ABROAD — STUDYING ABROAD AS AN OSTFALIA STUDENT

Ostfalia offers many opportunities to gain international experience.

ERASMUS+ PROGRAMME (Europe):
- Exchange semesters at European partner universities
- Monthly Erasmus+ grant (does not need to be repaid)
- Available to Bachelor and Master students, usually 1–2 semesters
- Partners in France, Spain, Poland, Switzerland, Turkey, Finland, Ireland, Italy, Portugal, Czech Republic

NON-EU EXCHANGES (DAAD PROMOS):
- Funded by DAAD PROMOS scholarship for exchanges outside Europe
- Partners in Brazil, Japan, Jordan, Canada, Morocco, Mexico, South Korea, Taiwan, Uruguay, USA

HOW TO APPLY — STEP BY STEP:
1. Contact the Auslandsbeauftragter (international coordinator) of your faculty, or the Internationalisierungs-Coach
2. Choose a partner university from the partner list
3. Submit your application via ISO (International Student Office)
4. Apply for funding: Erasmus+ grant (EU) or DAAD PROMOS (non-EU)
5. Select courses at the partner university
6. Talk to PAV (exam board) for credit recognition approval
7. Complete your Learning Agreement
8. Consider a language course at the Sprachenzentrum before departure

APPLICATION DEADLINES (approximate — confirm with ISO):
- For summer semester abroad: apply by October/November of the previous year
- For winter semester abroad: apply by April/May
