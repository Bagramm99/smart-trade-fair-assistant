INTERCULTURAL OFFERS — OSTFALIA LANGUAGE CENTRE

The Language Centre runs intercultural workshops and events alongside its language courses.

WHAT IS OFFERED:
- Intercultural communication workshops: understanding cultural differences in professional and academic contexts
- Cultural exchange events and meetups for international and German students
- Country-specific information sessions
- Workshops on studying and living in Germany as an international student

PURPOSE:
To help international students adapt to German academic culture and daily life, and to foster exchange between German and international students.

PARTICIPATION:
Open to all Ostfalia students. Most events are free. Some workshops require registration.
