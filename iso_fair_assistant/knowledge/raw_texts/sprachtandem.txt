LANGUAGE TANDEM — OSTFALIA

WHAT IS A LANGUAGE TANDEM?
Language tandem is a language exchange where two people meet regularly. One person's native language is the other's target language. Each practises the other's language for equal time.

ADVANTAGES:
1. Free — no cost to participate
2. Flexible — you and your partner choose when and where to meet
3. Lively — practise with a real native speaker
4. Balanced — both partners benefit equally
5. Autonomous — you set your own goals
6. Intercultural — learn about your partner's culture, not just the language
7. Complements formal courses — prepare/follow up on coursework together

HOW THE TANDEM WORKS:
- Meet regularly (e.g. once or twice a week), ideally on a fixed schedule
- Split time equally between both languages (e.g. 45 minutes each)
- When speaking your foreign language, your partner listens, helps, and gives feedback — but does not "teach"
- Stay in the agreed language even when it gets difficult — paraphrase, draw, point

HOW TO REGISTER:
Download registration form (PDF) from ostfalia.de/…/sprachtandem or register directly on the tandem webpage.
Send form by email to: sprachtandem@ostfalia.de
Re-register every semester even if previously registered.

AFTER REGISTRATION:
Once tandem partners are matched, you receive an invitation to the "Get Together" meeting at Am Exer 11, Wolfenbüttel — where you get tips on tandem learning.

CERTIFICATE:
To get a Tandem certificate you must:
1. Attend the Get-Together meeting at the start of semester
2. Meet your partner at least 8 times for at least 1 hour each
3. Document each meeting in the learning diary
Send completed learning diary to: sprachenzentrum@ostfalia.de
