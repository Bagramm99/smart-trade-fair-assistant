GERMAN AS A FOREIGN LANGUAGE (DaF) — OSTFALIA LANGUAGE CENTRE

LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS FOR DEGREE PROGRAMMES:
If you want to study at Ostfalia, you must provide proof of sufficient German language skills.
Accepted certificates:
- German Language Diploma of the Standing Conference of Ministers of Education (Level II)
- German Language Test for University Entrance (DSH) — at least DSH-2
- TestDaF — paper-based or digital, at least 16 points (level 4 each section)
- Assessment test after attending a preparatory college
- Goethe-Zertifikat C2
- telc exam "Deutsch C1 Hochschule"
- A school-leaving certificate recognised in Germany as university entrance qualification with German language proof

COURSES FOR INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS AND REFUGEES:
- DaF pre-courses: start ~2 weeks before semester, mornings, ~5 hours/day. Enrolled as a guest student.
- Regular DaF courses during semester: for international students wanting to improve German.
- Individual lecture attendance as guest student possible.
- DSH preparation course: successfully completing DSH (at least DSH-2) fulfils language requirements.
- Levels offered: A1 to C1 (availability depends on enrolled students each semester).

IMPORTANT: Visa note — courses at 4 hours/week only (standard DaF) cannot be used as basis for a study visa application.

FUN FACT: Over 120 million people worldwide speak German. It is the most widely spoken language in the EU. Around 80 million people are learning German as a foreign or second language.
