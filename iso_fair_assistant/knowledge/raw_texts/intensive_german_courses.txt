TOPIC: Intensive German courses during semester break at Ostfalia
============================================================

INTENSIVE GERMAN COURSES DURING THE LECTURE-FREE PERIOD

Intensive German language courses are offered during the semester breaks (lecture-free period).

FORMAT:
- Daily sessions, multiple hours per day
- Much faster progress than regular semester courses
- Available for various levels (A1 to B2/C1 depending on demand)

WHEN:
- Summer semester break: August/September
- Winter semester break: February/March
Exact dates published on the Language Centre website each semester.

WHO IS IT FOR?
- Incoming exchange students arriving before the semester and wanting a head start in German
- Students preparing for the DSH exam
- Anyone wanting an intensive German boost during the break

REGISTRATION:
Via the Language Centre directly. Places are limited — register early.

CONTACT: Language Centre — ostfalia.de/en/university/campus-facilities/language-centre/contact-us
