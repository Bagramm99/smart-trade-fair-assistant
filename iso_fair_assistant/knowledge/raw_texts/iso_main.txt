INTERNATIONAL STUDENT OFFICE (ISO) — OSTFALIA UNIVERSITY OF APPLIED SCIENCES

Welcome to the International Student Office (ISO) at Ostfalia!

International experiences open up new perspectives and shape academic and personal development. The ISO team provides comprehensive orientation and support for all questions relating to stays abroad during your studies. The ISO also supports exchange students from partner universities completing a temporary stay at Ostfalia.

MAIN SERVICES:
- Wege ins Ausland (Pathways Abroad): for Ostfalia students wanting to study abroad
- Wege an die Ostfalia (Your way to Ostfalia): for incoming exchange students
- Erasmus+ programme coordination
- DAAD PROMOS scholarships for non-EU exchanges
