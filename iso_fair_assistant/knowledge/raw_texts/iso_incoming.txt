INCOMING STUDENTS — FACTS FOR PARTNERS

International students are warmly welcome at Ostfalia!

NOMINATION PROCESS (for partner university coordinators):
1. Check your exchange agreement — which faculty is involved and how many students can be exchanged per year.
2. Send a nomination email to: incoming-iso@ostfalia.de including:
   - Full name of candidate
   - Date of birth
   - Place of birth
   - Nationality
   - Student's email address
   - Faculty / field of study at home university
   - Study level (Bachelor or Master)
   - Planned semester(s) at Ostfalia
   - Target faculty at Ostfalia
3. The ISO will contact the student directly with all steps for application and enrolment.
