STUDENT HOUSING — WOHNEN

STUDENT DORMITORIES (STUDENTENWOHNHEIM):
Managed by Studierendenwerk Ostniedersachsen.
Apply as early as possible — demand exceeds supply.
Apply via: stw-on.de
Cost: approximately €200–350 per month depending on room type and location.

PRIVATE ACCOMMODATION (WG — shared apartment):
Most students rent rooms in shared apartments.
Platforms: WG-Gesucht (wg-gesucht.de), ImmobilienScout24, eBay Kleinanzeigen
Cost: approximately €300–500 per month in Wolfenbüttel/Braunschweig area.

TEMPORARY ACCOMMODATION ON ARRIVAL:
If you need a place for your first days, contact ISO before arrival: incoming-iso@ostfalia.de

ADDRESS REGISTRATION (ANMELDUNG):
German law: register address within 2 weeks of moving in.
Required: passport + completed Anmeldeformular + Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (landlord confirmation letter)
In Wolfenbüttel: Stadthaus, Stadtmarkt 4, 38300 Wolfenbüttel.

TIPS:
- Start your housing search before arrival — good rooms go quickly
- Wolfenbüttel is a small, affordable city; Braunschweig is 15 min by train and has more options
- Some landlords prefer tenants with a German bank account — open one early
