CERTIFICATES AND LANGUAGE CERTIFICATES — OSTFALIA LANGUAGE CENTRE

The Language Centre offers official certificates and proof of language level.

AVAILABLE CERTIFICATES:
- Course completion certificates: proof that you attended and completed a language course at Ostfalia
- Tandem certificate: proof of tandem participation (requires 8 documented meetings — see language tandem page)
- DSH certificate: official proof of German language proficiency for university admission (DSH-1, DSH-2, DSH-3)

EXTERNAL CERTIFICATES ACCEPTED FOR ADMISSION:
The following are accepted as proof of German for degree programme admission:
- German Language Diploma Level II
- DSH-2 or DSH-3 (Ostfalia or other accredited institutions)
- TestDaF: at least 16 points (level 4 in all sections)
- Goethe-Zertifikat C2
- telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule

PLACEMENT TESTS:
Before enrolling in a course, take a placement test to find the right level.
