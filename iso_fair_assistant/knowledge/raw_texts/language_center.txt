LANGUAGE CENTRE (SPRACHENZENTRUM) — OSTFALIA

The Language Centre offers language courses, certificates, and intercultural programmes
for all Ostfalia students, staff, and international visitors.

COURSE OFFERINGS:
- German as a Foreign Language (DaF): multiple levels A1 to C1 for international students
- English, French, Spanish and other foreign languages
- Courses during the semester and during semester breaks (lecture-free periods)
- Intensive language courses during the lecture-free period
- Intercultural workshops and programmes
- Sprachtandem: free language exchange with a native speaker
- Official language certificates and proof of level
- Placement tests (Einstufungstests) to find the right level
