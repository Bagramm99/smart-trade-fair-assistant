STUDIERENDEN-SERVICE — GENERAL STUDENT ADMINISTRATION

This is the correct office for questions OUTSIDE the ISO's scope.

SERVICES:
- Enrollment (Immatrikulation) and re-enrollment (Rückmeldung / semester fees)
- Student ID cards (Ostfalia-Card)
- Enrollment certificates and official documents
- Study leave (Beurlaubung)
- Leaving the university (Exmatrikulation)
- General study advice: Zentrale Studienberatung

SUB-OFFICES:
- Immatrikulationsbüro: enrollment, student ID, certificates
- Zentrale Studienberatung: general study advice and switching programmes
