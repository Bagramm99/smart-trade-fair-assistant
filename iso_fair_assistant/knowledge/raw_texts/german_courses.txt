TOPIC: German courses during studies at Ostfalia for international students
============================================================

GERMAN COURSES DURING YOUR STUDIES

Regular German language courses run throughout the semester alongside your study programme.

COURSE LEVELS:
Courses are offered from A1 (complete beginner) to C1 (advanced), depending on student demand.
Not all levels are offered every semester.

REGISTRATION:
Take the placement test first to determine your level: ostfalia.de/en/…/placement-tests
Then register for the appropriate course at the Language Centre.

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Incoming exchange students wanting to learn everyday German during their stay
- Degree-seeking international students improving their language skills alongside their studies
- Refugees and asylum seekers (can join with teacher agreement)

COURSE FORMAT:
Typically 4 hours per week during the semester.
Classes in Wolfenbüttel campus, Am Exer building.
