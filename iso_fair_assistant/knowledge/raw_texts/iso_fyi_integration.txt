FYI — FOR YOUR INTEGRATION

The FYI programme supports international students in settling into life at Ostfalia and in Germany.

WHAT FYI OFFERS:
- Orientation events at the start of each semester
- Buddy programme pairing new international students with experienced students
- Intercultural workshops and social events
- Information on German bureaucracy: health insurance, address registration, bank accounts
- Support finding housing and navigating daily life in Wolfenbüttel/Braunschweig

HEALTH INSURANCE (KRANKENVERSICHERUNG):
All students enrolled in Germany must have statutory health insurance.
- EU/EEA students: European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) valid for short stays; enrol in German insurance for stays over 3 months.
- Non-EU students: must arrange German health insurance before enrollment.
- Recommended providers: TK (Techniker Krankenkasse), AOK, Barmer.

ADDRESS REGISTRATION (ANMELDUNG):
German law requires residents to register their address within 2 weeks of moving in.
Required: passport, completed Anmeldeformular, Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (landlord confirmation).
In Wolfenbüttel: Stadthaus, Stadtmarkt 4, 38300 Wolfenbüttel.

BANK ACCOUNT:
Many banks require a registered address first.
N26 and DKB offer fully online accounts — useful for new arrivals.
